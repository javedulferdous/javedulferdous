My research bridges **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** and **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** to design intelligent systems that make digital spaces **accessible, usable, and inclusive** for people with disabilities. I focus on:
- 🧩 Non-visual web interaction and screen-reader accessibility  
- 🤖 AI-driven adaptive interfaces for blind and neurodivergent users  
- 💬 Accessible online communication and conversational systems  
- 🌏 Disability and digital access in Global South contexts, especially Bangladesh  
- 🔐 Accessible security and privacy for screen-reader users (emerging direction)

### 🧠 Current Work
- 🧭 **Dissertation (completed):** *Proxy Interfaces for Enhancing Non-Visual Web Usability*  
- 🧩 **Projects:** Building Chrome extensions that integrate with local LLMs to improve blind users' interaction with web content, including e-commerce and consent management.  
- 🇧🇩 **Bangladesh:** Studying disability and digital access in Bangladesh, and running a scoping review of disability research in HCI4D and Global South contexts.
